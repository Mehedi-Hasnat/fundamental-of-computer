Introduction to Web Development

Web development is the process of creating and maintaining websites. It involves multiple aspects such as designing the structure of a webpage, organizing content, and applying styles to make the page visually attractive. The two most basic technologies used in web development are HTML and CSS. HTML is responsible for creating the structure of the webpage, while CSS is used to control the layout, colors, spacing, and overall appearance.

In modern times, websites play a crucial role in communication, education, and business. A well-designed website should be simple, user-friendly, and easy to navigate. Proper use of alignment, spacing, and typography ensures that the information is presented clearly. This assignment focuses on building a simple yet effective webpage using only HTML and CSS.

Procedure of Creating This Website

The first step of creating this website was planning the layout. I decided to place a header section at the top with a light gray background to highlight the title of the assignment. This helps in making the page look structured and professional.

Next, I created an information box to display important academic details such as department name, course name, student name, and merit position. This box was styled using CSS with a black border and rounded corners. The text inside the box was made bold and aligned in a single horizontal row to make it visually organized and attractive.

After that, I added multiple content sections to provide detailed explanations. These sections include information about web development and the steps followed to design this webpage. CSS was used to add spacing, alignment, and background colors to maintain a clean and readable layout. Special attention was given to margins, padding, and font styling to improve the overall presentation.

Importance of Design and Presentation

Design and presentation are very important in both web development and academic assignments. A clean and well-structured design makes the content easier to understand and more engaging for the reader. Even simple elements like proper spacing, consistent font usage, and alignment can significantly improve the quality of a webpage.

For this assignment, the goal was to keep the design simple yet informative. Additional explanatory content was included to ensure that the assignment does not appear too short when printed. This makes the final output more complete and suitable for submission in hard copy format.

Overall, this project demonstrates the fundamental concepts of HTML and CSS while also highlighting the importance of clear structure, proper styling, and detailed content in creating an effective webpage.
